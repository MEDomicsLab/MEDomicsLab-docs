# 🔬 Proofs of Concept

The following proof of concepts (_PoCs_) are a concrete demonstration of how our platform works. They demonstrate the feasibility and effectiveness of our modules in data modelling, in relation to a specific problem and that the desired process can be easily implemented with satisfactory quality.

We hope the PoCs will help users recognize the potential benefits and challenges of the proposed platform and its advantages over its counterparts.
